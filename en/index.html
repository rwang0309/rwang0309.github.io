Biography

Rui Wang is an Associate Professor at the School of Automation and Intelligence and the State Key Laboratory of Advanced Rail Autonomous Operation, Beijing Jiaotong University. She received her Bachelor degree (2011) and Master degree (2014) of Engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University, and her Ph.D. in Computer Science from INSA Toulouse (member of INSA Enginering School Group), France, in 2018. During her Ph.D., she conducted her research at LAAS-CNRS with the TSF group (renewed as TRUST) and also worked there as a researcher.

Her research focuses on trustworthy AI, uncertainty in machine learning, and intelligent system safety. She has led projects supported by the NSFC Young Scientists Fund, China State Railway Group, and a Ministry of Education collaborative education program, and contributed to national and regional research programs.
